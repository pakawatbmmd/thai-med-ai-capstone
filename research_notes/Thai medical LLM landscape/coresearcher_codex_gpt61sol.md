**Evidence-quality notes**

- **Exam evidence needs language/version labels.** A December 2024 preprint tested foreign models on **300 English-language mock Thai licensing questions**: GPT-4o **88.9%**, GPT-4 **83.3%**, Claude 3.5 Sonnet **80.1%**, Gemini 1.5 Pro **72.8%**. This does not validate Thai-language patient interaction. [Study](https://www.medrxiv.org/content/10.1101/2024.12.20.24319441v1.full).
- **Benchmark naming remains fragmented.** Eir’s **MedQa_Thai** dataset exists; a standardized, independently established benchmark specifically named **ThaiMedBench remains UNVERIFIED**. ThaiClinicBench evaluates summarization, while ThaiLLM med-IQ evaluates grounded information responses. [Dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/EIRTHAIMED/MedQa_Thai), [ThaiClinicBench](https://github.com/loolootech/ascribe), [med-IQ](https://huggingface.co/ThaiLLM/ThaiLLM-8B-MedApp).
- **Clinical evidence remains narrower than product adoption claims.** AppHerb has a peer-reviewed paper; Eir, CU-ICU, ASCRIBE and TSWAP supply research reports; major commercial scale/time-saving claims primarily come from vendors or partners. None of those sources establishes broad prospective clinical effectiveness. [AppHerb](https://doi.org/10.3390/ai6080170), [ASCRIBE](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.01234), [TSWAP](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.22917), [PresScribe](https://news.microsoft.com/source/asia/2025/05/20/bangkok-medical-software-utilizes-presscribe-ai-by-looloo-health-to-enhance-healthcare-services-across-35-hospitals-in-thailand-en/).
- **Coverage boundaries:** dedicated medical weights/scores for **Pathumma, KBTG and Chinda** remain **UNVERIFIED** in reviewed catalogs; general Thai LLM capability is insufficient evidence. Likewise, **MorDee/Doctor Raksa LLM-powered clinical functionality remains UNVERIFIED**; telemedicine availability alone does not establish it. [Pathumma](https://pathumma.in.th/), [ThaiLLM ecosystem](https://www.nstda.or.th/en/news/news-years-2026/thaillm.html), [iApp models](https://iapp.co.th/en/openmodels/openthai2p0), [LINE HEALTH announcement](https://linenewsroom.com/en/press-release/line-health-official-launch).
