# Council v9 brief: Feasible, approvable capstone. Ideas plus a committee roast

## Why this round exists
The student believes the committee would not approve the ideas from councils v3–v8. Those were tuned for frontier-ML novelty, not for this programme's bar. This round has **no topic constraint**. It judges ideas on **feasibility and likelihood of committee approval**, and each advisor plays the proposal committee: likely questions, attacks and verdict.

## The programme's bar (private materials are inlined separately; summary here)
- **Plan 2 professional MSc, one year.** The capstone is Independent Study I + II.
- **IS I:** an innovation project on a **real-world problem with potential for practical implementation**, with **stakeholder collaboration**.
- **IS II:** **prototype development for practical use, testing, validation, demonstration**.
- **Graduation:** a final oral exam plus a **national or international conference presentation**.
- The proposal follows a portfolio outline:
  - local problem with evidence;
  - users and stakeholders; current workflow;
  - why digital;
  - aim, objectives and scope;
  - evidence review and existing solutions;
  - PRD/MVP;
  - evaluation linked to success criteria;
  - implementation plan; ethics/IRB; risks; timeline.
- Last year's cohort (about 37 projects, catalogue inlined):
  - mostly off-the-shelf ML/LLM or app prototypes for a specific local clinical problem;
  - small or honest validations pass; rigour of validation distinguishes the best ones.
  - **Already done last year (avoid or clearly differentiate):** antimicrobial-resistance prediction; AI simulated patient (psychiatric interview); Thai drug terminology (TMT) mapping (twice); LLM EMR summarisation and NIHSS/TOAST extraction; CPR coaching by vision; diabetic retinopathy vs AMD; several stroke, dementia, psoriasis and sleep projects.
